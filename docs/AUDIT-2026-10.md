# NeroEconomy audit — October 2026

Baseline: `mod_version=0.2.0-alpha.1`, HEAD `8818250`, Core pin `1.13.0`, link schema 1.
This audit builds on `AUDIT.md` (August) and does not repeat it; the status of each August item
is in [August audit status](#august-audit-status).

## Summary

The ledger arithmetic is sound and the escrow model holds inside a single store, but four things
stop the mod doing what it says today:

1. **Contracts, deliveries and bounties cannot be posted with a usable target.** The command
   arguments reject `:` and `#`, and posting is command-only, so all three features are dead
   end to end.
2. **The companion-app Economy screen shows almost nothing against a real server.** The mod and
   the app disagree on every section's field names; the app's fixtures hide it.
3. **Any creative-mode player can mint credits and items into the live market** through
   `/neroeconomy gallery`, and wipe a 65×65×13 region with `gallery clear`.
4. **Money and goods live in four separately saved stores** with no shared transaction boundary,
   and the load guard replaces an unreadable store with an empty one and no backup.

There is also no supply accounting and no sink, so every faucet (starting balance, the craftable
Credit Chip, system jobs) is unbounded.

Counts: 3 Critical, 13 High, 27 Medium, 21 Low findings; 62 suggestions (`AUD-01` … `AUD-62`).

## Method

- Static read of every file under `common/`, the three loader modules, resources, build scripts,
  docs and wiki at the baseline commit. Nothing was changed while auditing.
- Both sides of the companion-app contract were read: this mod's link module, the bridge
  dispatcher in `../nerolink`, and the app's economy module and fixtures in `../nerolink-app`.
- Vanilla behaviour was checked against the patched 26.2 sources the build already downloads.
- Runtime behaviour was **not** exercised; findings marked *by reading* need the owner's smoke test
  to confirm. No finding below depends on a guess about player data.

Path shorthand: `J/` = `common/src/main/java/za/co/neroland/neroeconomy/`,
`R/` = `common/src/main/resources/`.

### Checked and sound

- Every serverbound handler requires the matching menu type, the same container id and
  `stillValid` within 8 blocks.
- Quantities and prices are clamped in the codecs; totals use exact arithmetic and reject overflow.
- The client price is only a stale-quote guard; the server re-quotes at execution.
- `quickMoveStack` returns empty in all three menus; hoppers see no shop slots.
- Two buyers on one listing settle under one monitor; the second gets `OUT_OF_STOCK`.
- Self-trade is refused on buy, fill, claim, bid and pay.
- Expiry refunds listings, offers, jobs and auctions; erasure runs obligations before the market.
- The erasure tombstone stops a starting balance being minted again.
- No log line at info or above carries a player, UUID, amount or transaction.
- No loader import in `common/`; both ServiceLoader files exist on all three loaders;
  `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` are byte-identical; the wiki has no private reference.
- All 91 lang keys used in code exist; every block and item has its model, texture, loot table
  and recipe.

## Store mutation order

What a save that persists one file but not the other does:

| Operation | Order | Split-save result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board buy | ledger debit → market credit claim, item claim, stock | Ledger only: buyer debited, nothing delivered. Market only: goods and seller proceeds appear from nothing |
| Shop buy | ledger transfer → market | Same pattern |
| Buy offer post | ledger debit → market | Ledger only: escrow destroyed. Market only: an unfunded offer pays out when filled |
| Collect credits | ledger deposit → market remove | Ledger only: the claim is collectable twice |
| Job post | ledger → obligations | As buy offer |
| Job fulfil | inventory → market claims → obligations | Market only: the job stays claimed, pays again and refunds at expiry |
| Job expire | market refund → obligations | Market only: the refund repeats |
| Auction bid | ledger debit → market refund → obligations | Three stores; a refunded bidder can still win |
| Auction settle | market proceeds and lot → obligations | Market only: lot and proceeds duplicated on restart |
