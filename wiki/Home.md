# NeroEconomy wiki

NeroEconomy supplies the persistent credits economy for Neroland servers. These pages describe the
current alpha release line for Minecraft 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 on Fabric, Forge and NeoForge. The
changelog in the repository lists what each version changed.

- [Credits and commands](Credits-and-Commands.md)
- [Bank Terminal](Bank-Terminal.md)
- [Player Shops](Player-Shops.md)
- [Markets and terminals](Markets-and-Terminals.md)
- [Contracts and delivery](Contracts-and-Delivery.md)
- [Bounties and auctions](Bounties-and-Auctions.md)
- [Credit Chip](Credit-Chip.md)
- [Privacy, history, export and erasure](Privacy-and-Erasure.md)
- [Configuration and administration](Configuration-and-Administration.md)
- [Public APIs and integrations](APIs-and-Integrations.md)
- [Architecture](Architecture.md)

Posting a job and creating an auction are done by command for now; everything else also has a
screen. Faction wallets, cross-server economies, and simulated supply/demand are deferred.
